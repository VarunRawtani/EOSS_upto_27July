Being contactable is the single clearest retention lever. Reachable customers migrated at roughly twice the non-contactable rate (0.71% vs 0.38%) and generated ₹2.02 Cr of the ₹2.46 Cr total — 82.2% of movement sales. Growing the contactable base is the clearest way to carry revenue through a store transition.
